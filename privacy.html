Detailed Information on the Processing of Personal Data

Personal Data is collected/processed for the following purposes and using the following services:

Google OAuth (only if you enable the Google Drive backup feature)

The services contained in this section enable the Application to store backups on the User's Google Drive. The Application will ask the User to allow the Application to:

  • “See your primary Google Account email address”: Needed to show you within the Application which Google Account you have connected.
  • “See your personal info, including any personal info you've made publicly available”: Needed to show your profile picture within the Application.
  • “Associate you with your personal info on Google”: Needed to technically connect your Google Account with the Application.
  • “See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”: Needed to save and delete backups from your Google Drive.

Any of this information will only be saved locally on your device within the Application.

Firebase Crashlytics

The services contained in this section enable Lucky Corp. to associate crash data with specific instances of the app Lucky Calendar.

Data Collected: Crashlytics Installation UUIDs, Crash traces, Breakpad minidump formatted data (NDK crashes only).

Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days before starting the process of removing it from live and backup systems.

Firebase Analytics

We use the third-party analytics tool Firebase Analytics to help us measure traffic and usage trends for the service. Firebase Analytics collects information such as how often users visit our services, what pages they visit, and what other pages they use prior to coming to our services. We use the information gotten from Firebase Analytics only to improve our services.

With your explicit opt-in (the Improving the model toggle in Backup & Data), the app additionally sends through Firebase Analytics an anonymized, aggregated summary of how accurate your luck forecasts were — overall quality numbers only (such as average error and calibration). This summary never includes your individual luck ratings, the dates of your entries, or your notes. It is disabled by default, requires your active consent, and can be switched off at any time.
